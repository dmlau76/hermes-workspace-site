Hermes Workspace Integration

A private, single-user automation tool.

Hermes Workspace Integration is a personal automation client operated by one individual for his own Google account. It is not a product, is not offered to the public, and has no other users.

It connects to Google APIs using OAuth 2.0 to:

  • read, search, and send email on the operator's own mailbox;
  • read and create events on the operator's own calendars;
  • read, create, and organise files in the operator's own Google Drive, and read and edit the operator's own Google Docs;
  • make encrypted backup copies of a self-hosted password manager into the operator's own Google Drive.

The integration runs on the operator's own hardware. When the operator requests AI-assisted tasks, relevant content may also be processed by the AI service providers configured for those tasks. Credentials are stored encrypted on that hardware and can be revoked at any time from the operator's Google Account permissions page.

See the privacy policy for detail on data handling, retention, and revocation.
